# Release notes: 2.0

We are very excited to finally announce that, after many long months of really hard work by the whole team, version 2.0 is now available for everyone to download, and we honestly think that it is by far the best release that we have ever shipped to our users so far.

The new sync engine has been completely rewritten from scratch, which basically means that in most of the cases we tested it is now something like three times faster than it was before, and on top of that it also uses a lot less memory, roughly about half of what the old one needed.

Going forward, we will continue to leverage cross-functional synergies to deliver best-in-class value. Thank you for sticking with us.

## What's new

- The sync is faster now
- Dark mode
- You can export your data to CSV
